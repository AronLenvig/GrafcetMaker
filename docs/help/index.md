# Help center

GrafcetMaker lets you draw GRAFCET charts (IEC 60848), check them and run them in a simulator,
right in your browser. These pages take you from your first chart to the advanced features.

## New to GrafcetMaker?

- Open an example from the start screen (**☰ › Start screen and examples**) and press
  <kbd>F5</kbd> to watch it run.
- Take the ten-step tour (**Help › Take the tour**) to learn where everything is.
- Choose your language with the flag at the bottom right.
- [Getting started](getting-started.md): draw, check and simulate a small tank-filling chart in
  about ten minutes. Start here.
- [GRAFCET basics](grafcet-basics.md): steps, transitions, receptivities, actions and the five
  rules that make a grafcet evolve. Read this if GRAFCET itself is new to you.

## Guides

- [Writing receptivities](expressions.md): AND, OR, NOT, rising ↑ and falling ↓ edges, timers,
  comparisons and step variables.
- [Actions](actions.md): continuous, conditional, stored on activation, deactivation or an event,
  and forcing orders.
- [Editing diagrams](editing.md): the keyboard and mouse moves that make drawing fast, branches,
  loops and automatic layout.
- [Variables](variables.md): inputs, outputs and internal variables, and exchanging them with Excel.
- [Simulation](simulation.md): run a grafcet, change inputs, follow timers, read the chronogram and
  replay scenarios.
- [Macro-steps, enclosing steps and forcing](hierarchy.md): structure large applications.
- [Files, saving and export](files.md): autosave, files, PDF, images and spreadsheets.

## Reference

- [Glossary](glossary.md): the standard GRAFCET terms used in the app.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): what each problem message means and how to fix it.
